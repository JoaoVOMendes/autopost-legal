Privacy Policy – Autopost

Last updated: 2026-11-11. Back to the Autopost homepage.

1. Who we are

Autopost is a personal tool operated by autopost-yt-tiktok ("the owner") on the owner's own computer. Contact: jhon.vm08@gmail.com.

2. Google user data we access

Only an OAuth access/refresh token that the owner grants with the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. Autopost does not read the owner's profile, channel details, playlists, comments or existing videos.

3. How the data is used

The token is used solely to upload videos, selected by the owner, to the owner's own YouTube channel, and to apply their title, description, tags and schedule.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in the operating system credential vault (for example Windows Credential Manager). Nothing is sent to the developer, to any server operated by the developer or to any third party other than the Google and TikTok APIs the owner chooses to use.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, share, transfer or disclose Google user data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising, analytics, profiling or to train AI models. The use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. TikTok

If the owner connects a TikTok account, TikTok tokens are likewise kept only in the local credential vault, and video files and captions are sent to TikTok only when the owner runs an upload.

7. Retention and deletion

Data stays on the owner's computer until deleted. The owner can revoke Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the stored credentials from the computer.

8. Children

Autopost is not directed to children.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest revision.
